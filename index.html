04 / RESEARCH & LEARNING

Questions worth investigating.

Technology should be evaluated not only by what it can do, but by whom it serves.

One of my research interests is demographic bias in face recognition systems: how performance can vary across demographic groups, how we measure those differences, and what responsible evaluation should look like.

I'm also interested in cloud architecture, generative AI, human behaviour and the relationship between technological progress and society.

Research status: ongoing exploration. Publications and formal outcomes will be added when available.
